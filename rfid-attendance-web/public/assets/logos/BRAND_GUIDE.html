CampusTrack Brand Guide

This kit is derived from the supplied CampusTrack logo artwork. Preserved exports retain the original appearance; true-vector recreations are approximations for scalable path-based use.

Primary logo

Use the light-background logo on white/off-white surfaces. Use the dark-theme logo on navy or dark photography. Do not stretch, rotate, skew, or arbitrarily recolor the primary lockup.

Clear space & sizing

Keep clear space around the full logo equal to at least the height of the “C” counter in Campus. Recommended minimum digital width: 180 px for the full lockup and 24 px for the icon.

Typography

The source artwork does not identify its original typeface. For supporting UI, use Inter or Manrope. For bold marketing headings, Nunito Sans ExtraBold is a close stylistic companion to the rounded wordmark.

Light / dark theming

Light: off-white background, navy text, teal actions. Dark: deep navy background, off-white text, teal actions.

Asset guidance

  • Preserved SVGs: exact supplied artwork wrapped in SVG for maximum fidelity.
  • Vector recreated SVGs: true vector shapes; useful for unlimited scaling but may differ slightly from the original wordmark.
  • Favicons: use icon-only artwork.
  • Android: use the supplied mipmaps/adaptive icon assets and light/dark color XML.
