# CampusTrack Analytics

Analytics is shared by administration and the portal. The entry permission is `view_analytics`; each domain also checks existing business permissions and current class relationships. Teacher/Student management grants remain class/personal in scope. Effective View As permissions are used for queries, counts, caches and exports. An Audit Trail grant alone grants no Analytics access.

## Setup and operation

After normal database setup, run `php scripts/install-analytics.php`. It installs the additive `20261003_analytics.sql` migration, source-history triggers, first-open/scanner columns, indexes and entry grants, then updates audit tracking and builds initial student facts and daily class aggregates. Repeat installation is safe and preserves the original baseline. Runtime deployment must install the foundation before releasing navigation/API changes.

The existing every-minute `scripts/notification-worker.php` cron runs Analytics when its last completed refresh is at least four minutes old. `php scripts/analytics-worker.php` runs it independently; `--rebuild` rebuilds attendance facts from retained sources/history. A database advisory lock prevents overlapping builds. All changed facts and the refresh revision commit together. A failed refresh retains the last committed facts and exposes a stale/error message. Inspect `analytics_state` and the private notification-worker completion marker to verify operation.

`analytics_history` is authoritative prospective business history. The state, change queue, occurrences, facts and cache are rebuildable operational tables and are excluded from Audit Trail row logging. History itself is audited with its payload redacted. History has no restrictive foreign keys and adds no deletion blockers. Before-delete tracking closes histories of cascading children because MySQL does not run their row triggers. Existing history/deletion protection remains enforced by existing business rules.

## Calculations and limitations

- Attendance grain: student/offering/date. Participation is `(Present + Late)/(Present + Late + Absent)` on finalized eligible records. Excused is separate. Missing expected records and provisional sessions are excluded. Unknown legacy eligibility is disclosed; legacy rates are recorded-observation rates, not verified roster completion.
- Late rate: `Late/(Present + Late)`. Minutes late require a captured meeting start. Expected rosters use enrollment at the scheduled start, department days off, teacher time off, semester dates and class-suspending event participation. Later changes do not rewrite earlier eligibility.
- Grades: finalized subject/component scores; no fabricated units, GPA scale, or combined lecture/laboratory weighting. Midterms/Endterms comparisons use paired finalized results with both enabled scores. The exact enabled terms, weights, passing threshold and course units are captured transactionally with new grade finalizations. Legacy finalizations have unknown policies. Passing rates exclude those unknown policies. `final` is a database field; its displayed grading-period label is Endterms.
- Campus activities: required audience at the activity start; Excused is excluded. Required checkout must be complete. Final rates apply to closed activities. Multi-day events remain one participant/event observation because that is the stored attendance grain. Optional participation is separate. Legacy audience-based rates are withheld. Academic filters use dated participant relationships, while the date range selects activities independently of semesters.
- Compliance: absences plus integer tardy equivalents by student, offering and captured conversion threshold. Unknown thresholds remain unknown. Analytics links to Compliance for resolution and does not issue sanctions.
- Resources: current weekly bookings and seat assignments within selected offerings/semester dates. Booking overlaps are interval intersections. These are not observed occupancy or historical estimates. Conflicts involving inaccessible offerings are not disclosed.
- Scanners: accepted/rejected authenticated attendance scan attempts. Registration is excluded. Uptime and pre-identification authentication failures are not measured. New rejection codes are explicit typed validation codes; old messages are not parsed into inferred categories.
- Communication: own sent messages only. A full authorized message open records its first server timestamp; bulk read/unread does not fabricate an open or erase it. Opening a message does not establish comprehension. Legacy reach and original first-open delays are withheld. Only messages sent after the tracking baseline contribute to reach rates; opens after the selected period end are excluded.
- Rates sum numerators/denominators, not percentages. Trend attention requires a ≥5-point decline, ≥5 recorded academic days and ≥20 eligible observations in both adjacent five-day windows. It is descriptive, not a significance test or causal prediction. Cohort changes may affect comparisons.

Historical placement/enrollment/schedule data before the installation baseline cannot be reconstructed accurately. Baseline grade policies are intentionally not presented as past policy. Missing legacy data is not backfilled from current profiles. Current metadata labels identify legacy records and are described as current labels. DDL/TRUNCATE changes require controlled maintenance and reinstalling history/audit tracking.

## API and assets

Read-only `GET /api/analytics.php`: `section`, `from`, `to`, academic IDs, `mode=summary|records|export`, `record_page`, `limit`, allowlisted `sort`, `direction`, `q`, `panel`, `refresh=1`. Invalid ranges produce 422; forbidden scopes 403; expired sessions 401. Responses are no-store JSON without SQL details. CSV applies the same access rules and neutralizes spreadsheet formulas. Refresh reads facts and never submits mutations. Cache entries are scoped by user, effective roles/permissions, filters, metric version and fact revision, the live dependency-change revision, with a 60-second TTL. Revoked class access is checked again on every request.

`npm ci` and `npm run build:analytics` rebuild the self-hosted Apache ECharts 6.1.0 SVG-only bundle. Only Analytics loads it. Theme/accent changes redraw charts; data tables, record drawers, keyboard controls and print views provide alternative access. Chart point/row limits are displayed; complete tables/drilldowns remain available.

CSV exports include a readable report heading, permitted filter labels, calculation and coverage notes, and a labeled records table. Scheduling & resources defaults to class meeting schedules; a drawer export retains its selected panel and record search. Exports include every record page, preserve the requested attendance sort, and escape formulas in metadata as well as records. Export links remain unavailable during loading or failed requests so a previous section cannot be downloaded accidentally. Blank data cells mean unavailable, never an assumed zero.

Filters are grouped into Reporting period and Academic filters, with the shared searchable dropdowns for every available selection, including short option lists. Subject, program, department, and offering labels include descriptive names; offerings identify their component. Roles without applicable academic options see only the period controls. Export and Print view share the page heading, adapt to mobile, and stay out of printed reports. CSV downloads bypass the navigation skeleton.

## Validation

`python tests/analytics_integration.py` uses a disposable MariaDB database on port 3340 and headless Edge. It checks historical enrollment and timetable changes, immutable policy capture during corrections, source and refresh rollback, captured activity audiences and cascade deletion, expected observations, multi-role scoping, View As, first-open semantics, all sections, CSV, pagination, invalid inputs, keyboard operation, mobile themes and 100,000-observation performance. No production records are used for destructive fixtures.

Fixture measurements (Windows local MariaDB, 100,000 attendance observations): representative summaries 45 ms uncached / 34 ms cached; the multi-year filtered summary 57 ms. Indexed student/date retrieval was checked with EXPLAIN. These are test-environment measurements; production timing is recorded separately during rollout.

`python tests/analytics_exports_integration.py` checks readable CSVs in all permitted domains, full pagination, formula escaping, filtered schedule and panel downloads, stale-link prevention during failed section changes, Student/Teacher scope, View As, and expired sessions in disposable fixtures on port 3340.

Production smoke test, 2026-10-03: all nine sections, role previews, direct attendance navigation, CSV, mobile themes, keyboard handling and expired sessions passed. Summary requests measured 255?282 ms including network latency. The existing cron completion marker confirmed scheduled Analytics refresh; 87 analytics triggers and 168 audit triggers cover the installed business tables. The original historical baseline is 2026-10-03 15:03:55 campus time.
