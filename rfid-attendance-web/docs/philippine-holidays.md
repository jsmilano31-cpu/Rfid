The shared campus and attendance calendars load Philippine holidays through authenticated, read-only `GET /api/holidays.php?year=2026`. The server uses [PhilippineHolidayAPI](https://github.com/surelle-ha/PhilippineHolidayAPI) at its public deployment's `GET /holiday/{year}` route. `PHILIPPINE_HOLIDAY_API_URL` can point to a separately maintained HTTPS deployment. CampusTrack never requests upstream scraping, updates, or deletion during a calendar visit.

Successful annual lists are cached privately for 24 hours. Failed/empty responses have a five-minute retry interval; the last saved list remains available with a warning. For 2026, dated national declarations provide verified classifications and missing holidays. The source URLs and verification date are recorded in `database/data/philippine-holidays-2026.json`. The upstream currently exposes only event/date/day, so unverified classifications remain explicitly unavailable. New years require a usable upstream snapshot; the calendar explains missing coverage rather than projecting this year's dates. Later declarations require updating the dated verification file.

National working/non-working status and campus no-class policy are separate. Clicking a holiday opens an accessible dialog. The migration restores `manage_academic_calendar` for the built-in Administrator when it is missing; it grants no other role automatically. `manage_academic_calendar` permits saving a campus-wide no-class decision with CSRF protection; all other authenticated users can inspect the holiday and the decision. Past dates are read-only, matching prospective scheduling rules. Existing attendance records and finalized sessions prevent a new no-class decision. Overrides can be removed for current/future dates; department days off and teacher time off still apply.

The date policy is enforced by academic attendance recording/finalization and room scanner scheduling. It does not cancel events or recollections. Policy changes, Audit Trail entries, and analytics history commit together. Historical analytics use the decision in effect at the occurrence, preserving previous observations. Account deletion can clear an actor reference without deleting the campus decision.

Install with `php scripts/install-analytics.php` (also installs the additive holiday migration and audit/history triggers). `python scripts/deploy-holidays.py` lists scoped files; `--deploy` performs backups, drift checks, additive installation, original-baseline verification, byte verification and temporary inspector cleanup. The deployment creates no real campus closures. Validate using `python tests/holiday_calendar_integration.py` against the disposable MariaDB on port 3340; never point this fixture at production.
